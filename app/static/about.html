About proAmazingSpider

Spider Solitaire for Android—clear HUD, color-coded suits, and quick actions. Built for touch and published on Google Play by SuperAce.

Why we built it

We wanted a faithful Spider experience on phones: ten columns, readable cards, and honest feedback in the header (Moves, Stack, Done). Online mode keeps sessions and saved games on our servers so you can pick up where you left off—see our Privacy Policy for details.

What you will notice

Blue-and-white patterned backs, green clubs, deep blue spades, and orange diamonds. A brown felt-style table with a light Amazing Spider watermark. A bottom row of actions—New, Deal, Solve, Undo, and Help—each with its own color so muscle memory kicks in fast.

Session-friendly

No account is required to play. When you use online mode, a random session identifier ties your moves to stored game state on our servers—deleted after about an hour of inactivity unless you keep playing.
