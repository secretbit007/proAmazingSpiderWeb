proAmazingSpider

Spider Solitaire on your phone—ten columns, familiar rules, and a warm table-style board. Track Moves, Stack, and progress Done right in the header.

Color-coded suits (green clubs, blue spades, orange diamonds), crisp card backs, and quick actions: New, Deal, Solve, Undo, and Help.

Ten columns, real Spider

Play on a full tableau with face-down stacks and clear face-up cards—just like classic Spider Solitaire on the desktop.

Readable suit colors

Clubs in green, spades in deep blue, diamonds in orange—easy to scan when you are building runs.

Controls at your thumb

New, Deal, Solve, Undo, and Help are one tap away on a friendly bottom toolbar.

Stay oriented with a clear HUD

The top bar keeps the essentials visible: Moves you have taken, Stack progress (for example 5/5), and how many completed sequences are Done out of eight—so you always know how close you are to finishing the deal.

Online mode talks to our servers so your session and saved layout can carry across requests—see the Privacy Policy for how we handle session data.

Actions that match how you play

New starts fresh, Deal pulls from the stock, Solve helps when you want a nudge, Undo walks back a move, and Help explains the rules. Each control uses a bold color so it is easy to hit the right one without looking away from the table.

The board uses a soft brown felt-style backdrop with a subtle Amazing Spider watermark—focused on the cards, not clutter.
